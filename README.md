# CITC Club Website – NIT Agartala

The official website of the Civil Innovation and Technology Cell (CITC), NIT Agartala. The website serves as the primary platform for showcasing the club's activities, team members, projects, achievements, placement records, blogs, and important announcements.

## Live Website

Website Link - https://citc-website-csph.onrender.com/

## Features

- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Team member profiles and organizational structure
- Placement and achievement showcase
- Blog and article management
- Contact and communication section
- Regular content updates and maintenance
- Optimized user experience and website performance
- Accessible and easy-to-navigate interface

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Project Overview

As the WebOps Team Lead of CITC, NIT Agartala, I was responsible for maintaining, updating, and improving the club's official website. The website acts as a central hub for students, faculty, and visitors to stay informed about the club's initiatives, events, technical activities, and achievements.

My work involved implementing updates across multiple sections of the website, including team member information, placement records, blogs, and contact details. I collaborated closely with the design and content teams to ensure that information remained accurate, up-to-date, and visually consistent.

Special attention was given to responsiveness, accessibility, and performance optimization to provide a smooth browsing experience across different devices and screen sizes.

## Key Contributions

- Led the WebOps team responsible for website maintenance and enhancements
- Updated and managed team, placement, blog, and contact sections
- Improved responsiveness and cross-device compatibility
- Ensured content accuracy through collaboration with multiple teams
- Maintained website stability and performance through regular updates
